Consultation & Skin Analysis

Every great result starts with understanding your skin. Our consultation process — powered by our Advanced Skin Analyzer Machine — gives us a clinical-grade picture of your skin before a single treatment begins.

🤖 Our Advanced Skin Analyzer

Our clinic uses an AI-powered multi-spectrum skin analyzer that sees beneath the skin surface to detect pigmentation, hydration levels, pore size, acne spots, wrinkles, UV damage and more. The machine generates a personalised skin health report that guides your treatment plan and tracks your progress. This technology sets us apart from most clinics in Port Harcourt.
