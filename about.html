About Our Clinic

Xotic Queens is a licensed clinical medspa in Port Harcourt specializing in
CPD & ITEC certified corrective skincare treatments for dark skin.

Our Mission is to Revitalize Your Skin & Confidence

At Xotic Queens Skincare & Medspa, we believe that radiant, healthy skin is a powerful source of self-confidence. Led by our clinical director, Queen Uboegbulam, we are dedicated to offering world-class corrective skincare treatments specifically tailored to address hyperpigmentation, acne, and anti-aging needs. We combine the latest clinical technologies with international certifications (CPD & ITEC) to deliver safe, effective, and transformative results for our valued clients.

  • We strive to bring out the best in dark skin
  • Bespoke plans customized to your unique goals
  • Certified professionals with global qualifications
  • Safety first—zero PIH risk on Fitzpatrick IV-VI skin

Why Choose Xotic Queens?

We pride ourselves on offering bespoke treatments that respect your skin's unique chemistry. Our clinic specializes in treating melanin-rich skin safely, avoiding the risks of hyperpigmentation associated with standard aggressive clinical treatments.

Care About Client’s Skin Goals

Every clinical journey begins with an in-depth skin analysis and a custom plan designed to achieve your specific targets.

Post-Treatment Support
